Memory here means device memory: each GPU or TPU has its own (for example 80 GB of HBM on an H100), separate from the computer's system RAM. JAX arrays live there by default (memory_kind=device). The 8 simulated CPU devices used to produce the outputs share system RAM, but the layout is the same.
